You draw your divine blade from its scabbard and a halo of golden light irradiates the throne hall. Naar bellows with a terrible rage. He is mortally aghast that such a holy weapon should be unsheathed in his inner sanctum, and in his rage he summons yet a further horde of Crypt Spawn to attack and overwhelm you. 4

Crypt Spawn Horde: COMBAT SKILL 55   ENDURANCE 40

If you win this combat, turn to 350.
